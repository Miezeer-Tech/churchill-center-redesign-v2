FAQ

Questions, answered.

General information only; it is not medical advice. Results vary from patient to patient.

How can I boost my immune system quickly?

Cut sugar and alcohol, eat organic whole foods, walk or do aerobic exercise 30–60 minutes most days, and identify any food sensitivities. Dr. Churchill may also recommend supplements tailored to you.

What are signs of a weak immune system?

Fatigue, frequent or lingering illness, allergies and gut symptoms. Blood tests can check white cells and inflammation.

How long does it take to boost the immune system?

Typically 4–8 weeks, depending on your plan and how closely you follow it.

How much does regenerative therapy cost?

It depends on the area treated and the approach. Please contact the office for a personal quote.

Is stem cell treatment for knees FDA approved?

No. Regenerative treatments are not FDA approved for this use. Imaging is advised first, since some knees may still need replacement.

Does insurance cover regenerative medicine?

Generally no. Coverage may change, so check with your insurer.

Are consultations really free?

Yes. We offer a free 15-minute online consultation by Zoom.
