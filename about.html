About us

Welcome to The Churchill Center.

Established in 2005, we offer a healing-oriented approach to integrative medicine that looks at the whole person: body, mind and spirit.

Prevention first.

We focus on optimal health, wellness and the prevention of illness. The most advanced treatments and pharmaceutical prescriptions come after, not before.

We explore every aspect of your lifestyle, whether you visit our Arcadia office, meet us online, or receive concierge-style care in the comfort of your home.

What guides our care.

Whole-person

Body, mind and spirit, not only the illness.

Prevention

Long-term wellness before last-resort treatment.

Convenience

Office, online or at-home visits.
